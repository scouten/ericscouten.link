Aphera is a native Apple Silicon RAW photo editor for Mac, built around a from-scratch imaging pipeline, film-inspired color science, and a keyboard-driven workflow that stays responsive even on 100MP files. It's positioned less as a Lightroom clone and more as a fast, project-based tool for photographers who want a signature look applied with one keystroke. Free 14-day trial, requires macOS 15+.
