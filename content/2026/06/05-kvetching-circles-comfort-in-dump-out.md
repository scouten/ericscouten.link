Brené Brown and Adam Grant explore why toughness and kindness aren't opposites but rather complementary qualities that need each other. A thoughtful conversation about how effective leadership and relationships require both directness and compassion.

At 21:58 (linked), Adam introduces the concept of a _kvetching circle,_  that describes a model for thinking through various people’s roles in responding to a tragedy or difficult circumstance. **Comfort in, dump out.**

BTW this new podcast, _The Curiousity Shop,_ is fast becoming one of my favorites. I’ve been listening since they launched a few months ago.
