via [El Perro Negro (@elperronegro@shmg.online)](https://shmg.online/@elperronegro/117297136735175195): For 9 months I have been using RapidRaw as part of my RAW image workflow. So much so that Rawtherapee and Lightroom have fallen by the wayside.

RapidRAW is a non-destructive, GPU-accelerated RAW photo editor that ships in about 20 MB — smaller than many of the images it edits. Built with Rust and Tauri by a solo developer, it offers AI masking, presets, batch edits, and optional generative tools that can run locally, via your own ComfyUI server, or in the cloud.
