A Siri shortcut that captures highlights from whatever you're listening to in Overcast, transcribes and summarizes the surrounding audio, and syncs the result to Readwise (and from there to Obsidian, Notion, Roam, etc.) or email. "Hey Siri, highlight" is exactly the interaction I want when a good idea goes by while my hands are busy.
