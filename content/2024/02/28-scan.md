Greg Pierce (of Drafts fame) has released Simple Scan, an iPhone and iPad app that does exactly one thing: scan a document to PDF or image and send it where you want. No cloud accounts, no expense report workflows, no ads — just a couple of options and a big Scan Document button, which is precisely right for those of us who scan a receipt once a month.
