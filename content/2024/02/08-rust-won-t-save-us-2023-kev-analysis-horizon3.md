Horizon3's analysis of the 2023 CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog finds that memory-safety bugs account for a relatively small share of what attackers actually exploit; injection flaws, authentication bypasses, and logic errors dominate. A useful reality check for those of us who reach for Rust as a security panacea — memory safety closes one door, but plenty of others remain wide open.
