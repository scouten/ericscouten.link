Unread, long a favorite RSS client on iOS and iPadOS, has finally landed on the Mac with version 4.0 — a fully native AppKit/SwiftUI app that's fast, customizable, and supports Feedbin and other sync services. Nice to see continued investment in native Mac RSS readers as the open web and fediverse feeds keep growing.
