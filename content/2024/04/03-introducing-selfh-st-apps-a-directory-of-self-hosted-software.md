via [Ethan Sholly (@shollyethan@fosstodon.org)](https://fosstodon.org/@shollyethan/112207107796883634): Today I'm launching a project I've been working on for several months - a browsable directory of self-hosted software:

Ethan Sholly of the This Week in Self-Hosted newsletter has published his entire database of self-hosted software projects as a browsable directory. You can filter by tags and by the commercial services they replace, and sort by repo stars, recent development activity, or name — with the whole thing refreshed daily from the database he maintains for the newsletter.
