Someone fed the text of the MIT License into Suno's AI music generator with the prompt "sad girl with piano" and got back a genuinely affecting piano ballad. The highlight: it nails "WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY" with an Imogen Heap-style glissando, then immediately mangles "FITNESS" into "fistiff."
