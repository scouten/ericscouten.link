Luca Palmieri fills a real gap in the Rust adoption literature: not how to convince your org to try Rust, but what to do once you've decided. His advice to *not* hire Rust specialists for your first project — and instead assess and upskill the team you already have — rings true, as does the warning about stranding a team of frustrated Rust experts if the bet doesn't pan out.
