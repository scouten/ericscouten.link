Andrew Lokenauth walks through the "Buy-Borrow-Die" strategy: the wealthy hold appreciating assets, borrow against them at low rates instead of selling (avoiding capital gains), and pass the assets on with a stepped-up basis so the gains are never taxed. A concise explanation of a tax loophole that's hiding in plain sight.
