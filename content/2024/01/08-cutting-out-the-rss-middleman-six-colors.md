Dan Moren drops Feedly and moves his RSS subscriptions into Reeder's own iCloud-synced feed list, cutting out the sync service entirely. The bigger point: RSS and OPML remain one of the few places where moving your data between apps and services takes about a minute — a portability standard the rest of the web could learn from.
