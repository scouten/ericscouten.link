Kev Quirk walks through EchoFeed, Robb Knight's managed service that watches an RSS feed and automatically cross-posts new entries to Mastodon, Bluesky, Micro.blog, GitHub, Discord, or a webhook. It's free for a single feed and $25/year for more (with a discount for omg.lol members), making it a cheap, set-and-forget alternative to Micro.blog's cross-posting service.
