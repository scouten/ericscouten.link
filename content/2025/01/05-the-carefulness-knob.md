Lorin Hochstein stages a short play in which an engineering manager discovers the "carefulness knob" — the dial a team implicitly turns when deciding how much testing and process rigor a change deserves. The punchline is that cranking it to maximum doesn't eliminate incidents, it just pushes delivery dates out exponentially, and teams are far worse at estimating risk than anyone would like to admit.
